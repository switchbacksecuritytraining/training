Saturday 26 · One day · Core skills

TCP/IP Deep Dive with Wireshark

Chris Greer

Price TBC per seat · one day

A core skills foundation in packet capture and protocol analysis. Where to capture in a switched network, capture filters against display filters, the core protocols, the TCP handshake and iRTT, receive and congestion windows, an introduction to TLS decryption, and the stream and I/O graphs that make a performance problem visible to people who don't read packets.

Sunday 27 · One day · Forensics

Threat Hunting with Wireshark

Chris Greer

Price TBC per seat · one day

Wireshark for cybersecurity forensics. Where IDS and IPS coverage ends, how to think like the attacker, capturing for the hunt, scan and botnet traffic, mapping what you find to MITRE ATT&CK, spotting web enumeration, Tshark for automation, and working alongside Snort and Suricata.
